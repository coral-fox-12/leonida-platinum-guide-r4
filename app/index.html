    Roadmap to the platinum

    1. Play the story first. The opening, Chapters 1–4 and the final mission earn 6 trophies by themselves; six more come from story missions on the way, and the strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each end in one. The story unlocks the map, the activities and the money everything else needs.
    2. Watch the one missable. Positive Mental Attitude wants you to meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 of them. They are easy to walk past and never come back as a checklist, so stop at each one as you travel instead of saving it for cleanup.
    3. Try every activity once. Master of None needs a single attempt at each of the 9 activity types. While you are out there, start the long counts: 250 stunts, 20 different race wins, 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 Top Fun drone-race locations.
    4. Collect while you explore. Card sets, 100 vehicles, 52 areas of Leonida and 10 Scooter Bros registrations all reward moving through the state with your eyes open. The 100 WAiNK spots and the 5 bank vaults are the long tail — fold them into free-roam sessions.
    5. Clean up, then 100%. Finish the remaining challenges, the stranger strands and the money goals ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages). A Long Road — 100% completion — is the last gold before the platinum pops.
